## English

**What this is.** DigiClassroom is testing whether a computer can read students' handwriting and suggest marks, so that teachers can mark faster. To test it fairly we need real handwriting from adults who copy short texts by hand.

**What you will do.** Copy short given texts by hand, on a writing pad (a tablet with a pen) or on paper that we photograph. About `[N]` texts, about `[M]` minutes in all.

**What we keep.** The image and strokes of your writing, how long you took, and the kind of device. We do **not** keep your name with the writing: you are known by a **pseudonym** (`[pseudonym]`). Your account email is linked to your consent record only so that we can check it; only the platform owner can see that link.

**How it is used (tick what you agree to).**
- ☐ **Evaluation:** to test and compare handwriting readers and markers.
- ☐ **Shadow mode:** to run a candidate reader or marker in the background in a test setting.
- ☐ **Report:** to include summary figures (never your writing or your name) in a report about how well the system works.

**Who sees your writing.** The people evaluating the system, by pseudonym. If a computer service is used to read it, only a de-identified image is sent. Your writing is never shown to students or teachers of any school as yours.

**How long.** We keep it for `[period]`, then delete it.

**Your rights.** This is voluntary and nothing happens to you if you say no. You may withdraw at any time by telling `[contact]`. After you withdraw, your writing is left out of every new version of the evaluation sets, and tasks that have not been done are stopped. Figures already published as totals cannot be recalculated. `[Legal review: confirm this wording.]`
