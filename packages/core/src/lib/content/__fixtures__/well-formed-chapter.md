**Wants and Resources**

People want many different things, and the list of things they want grows longer
as their circumstances change. The resources available to satisfy those wants do
not grow at the same pace, and that gap is where economics begins.

[GLOSSARY TERM | Page 201]
Term: Resources
Definition: Factors used for the production of goods and services. These can be
natural, like water and coal, or human-made, like capital and technology.
[/GLOSSARY TERM]

Every household divides what it has between the things it needs today and the
things it hopes to buy later. Economists call the choice between those two uses
of the same resource an alternative use.

**Production Possibilities**

A farmer with a fixed piece of land must decide how much of it to plant with
barley and how much with wheat. Planting more of one leaves less land for the
other.

**Summary - Chapter 3: Every Block Type, Well Formed**

Human wants are unlimited while the resources available to satisfy them are
limited. Because a resource put to one use cannot be put to another at the same
time, every use of a resource has an alternative that was given up.
